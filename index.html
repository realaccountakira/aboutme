About

People-first analytics, engineered for clarity.

I sit at the intersection of HR, data, and systems — making sure the numbers are right, the systems run smoothly, and the story is easy to understand.

My focus is always the same: accurate data, reliable systems, and insights that help leaders make confident, fair decisions.
