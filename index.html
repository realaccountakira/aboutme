About

People-first analytics, engineered for clarity.

I sit at the intersection of HR, data, and systems — making sure the numbers are right, the systems run smoothly, and the story is easy to understand.

As a Senior Data Analyst and HRIS specialist, I design the pipelines and dashboards that turn raw workforce data into a clear picture of headcount, retention, compensation equity, and operational health.

On the systems side, I configure and maintain HRIS platforms, automate manual processes, safeguard data integrity, and partner with HR, Finance, and IT to keep everything aligned and audit-ready.

My focus is always the same: accurate data, reliable systems, and insights that help leaders make confident, fair decisions.
